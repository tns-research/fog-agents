## LinkedIn post (820/3000 characters)

A VC spends 3 min 44 on a seed deck on average, and only 58% of decks get read to the last page.

In March 2026 DocSend updated its study of how investors read seed decks, section by section. The business model comes first with 64 seconds, ahead of the product (59 s) and traction (40 s). The "Why now" slide comes last with 23 seconds.

Two things to change in your next deck. Start with the section VCs read the longest: your pricing, gross margin, customer acquisition cost and payback period, as soon as you measure them. Then work on the opening, since 42% of decks are not read to the end: purpose in one sentence, problem, solution, then market.
