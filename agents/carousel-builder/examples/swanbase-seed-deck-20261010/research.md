## Caveats found during research

- The section times do not add up to 3 min 44 s (their sum is 7 min 30 s): each average is computed on the decks that contain the section. The chart carries that note under it.
- The swanbase guide still quotes the 2022 figure (3 min 20 s per deck). DocSend updated it to 3 min 44 s on 2026-03-10. The carousel uses the current figure; the guide should be updated (the carousel does not quote the guide's number).
- "42 %" in the captions is computed from the source (100 % minus 58 %), not quoted.

## Angles considered (propose-angles)

1. **Numerical lens, chosen**: "A VC reads your seed deck in 3 min 44", where the time goes section by section, then what to fix first. Strongest data, answers the reader's question in the cover.
2. Contrarian: "Your product slide is not the one VCs read the longest". Rejected: 64 s versus 59 s is too close to carry a contrarian claim.
3. How-to: "Open your deck with these four sections". Kept as one slide of angle 1 rather than a carousel: one sourced sentence cannot hold 8 slides.
4. Story: a founder's deck read in DocSend analytics. Rejected: no real case with consent, and swanbase does not invent practices.
