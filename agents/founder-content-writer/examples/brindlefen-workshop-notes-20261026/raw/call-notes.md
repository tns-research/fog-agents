Notes after a call with a pilot workshop, typed by the founder. Fictional fixture.

The owner told me the first week felt slower, because the team was entering bikes they would normally just remember. The second week was when the status texts started going out on their own, and the phone calmed down enough that he noticed it.

He does not care about the dashboard. He cares that the text goes out when the bike moves to the finished hook, without anyone touching a screen with greasy hands.

What he asked for next: a way to say a part is on order, because that is the question customers ask most.
