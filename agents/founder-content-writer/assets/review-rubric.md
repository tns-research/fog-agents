# Review rubric: a batch of founder posts

Used by the fresh reviewer on `review/critique-packet.md`. Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2 per post. Pass: 9 or more out of 12 on every post and no criterion at 0. The scripts already proved the passages exist, the numbers trace and the limits hold: the reviewer judges what a script cannot.

| # | Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fidelity | every sentence says what the cited passages say, or is plainly the founder's opinion drawn from them | one sentence goes a step further than its passage | an event, a result or a detail that no passage holds |
| 2 | Voice | reads like the raw material and the voice pack: same words, same length of sentence, same way of speaking to the reader | correct, but smoother or more polished than the founder | reads generated, or breaks a voice rule or a recorded decision |
| 3 | First line | the idea stands in the first line, and a reader in the founder's market stops on it | clear, and could open anyone's post | context or a greeting; the idea comes later |
| 4 | One idea, useful | one idea, developed, and the reader leaves with something to look at or do | one idea, thin | two ideas, or nothing to keep |
| 5 | Native form | the LinkedIn post reads as a post and the tweet stands alone as one thought | the tweet is a cut of the LinkedIn post | a body written for the other network |
| 6 | Honesty of proof | numbers and names read as the proof list says them, market quotes stay strangers' words | a claim is correct but leans on a vague word | a quote reads as a customer, or a claim reads stronger than its source |

Also return one line on the batch: do the hooks and angles feel varied to a reader who sees all the posts in one week?

Return per post: the scores, the located problems (`<ref>.<network>`, the sentence), one proposed fix each. No rewriting: the producer revises once through `posts-spec.json`.
