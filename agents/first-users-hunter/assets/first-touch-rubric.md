# First-touch review rubric

Used by `skills/drafting-first-touch/SKILL.md` (self-check, Step 5) and by the fresh reviewer of the people file ([`shared/skills/reviewing-with-fresh-eyes/SKILL.md`](../../../shared/skills/reviewing-with-fresh-eyes/SKILL.md)). Score each person's row 0 to 2 per criterion. A row ships with every criterion at 1 or more and a total of 12 or more out of 16; otherwise it is revised once, then dropped with its reason.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Answers the signal | generic, could be sent to anyone | touches the topic | answers the person's actual point, visible without context |
| 2 | Sits where the signal was said | touch 1 is a cold DM while a public reply was possible | public, but in another thread | a reply in the signal's own thread, or the documented reason why not |
| 3 | No pitch in touch 1 | offer, link or call request | offer named without the person asking for it | nothing to sell, or the offer named only because the person asked for exactly it |
| 4 | Facts are sourced | a fact about the person or the offer with no source | a source exists but is not the cached page | every fact about the person is on a cached URL, every fact about the offer in project-context |
| 5 | Language and register | not the person's language | right language, register off | the person's language and, in French, their `tu` or `vous` |
| 6 | Full sentences, plain words | fragments, bullet lines, slogans | some stock phrases | prose a peer would write, 0 BLOCK at the writing gate |
| 7 | Touch 2 | missing where a channel exists, or two asks | one ask but heavy | recalls touch 1, one clause on the sender, at most one small ask |
| 8 | Fit reason | missing or a title only ("CEO at X") | plausible, weak source | names what the person said or did, with its URL |

Blocking regardless of score: an invented proof, number, client or result; an equity figure; a guessed email; a message that exposes a public complaint back to its author; a real founder first name in a signature instead of `{{sender_first_name}}`.
