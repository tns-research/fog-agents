# Review rubric: variants of a post

Used by the fresh reviewer at the end of Step 4, on the review packet, `posts.json` and the deliverable. Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2 per post. Pass: 9 or more out of 12 and no criterion at 0. Script checks (lint, writing gate, limits) are already green: the reviewer judges what a script cannot.

| # | Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fidelity | every variant says what the deliverable says, no claim added or sharpened | one phrasing goes a step beyond the deliverable | a number, name or result absent from the deliverable |
| 2 | Native form | each network gets its own first line and length (one tweet that stands alone, a caption written for swiping) | two variants are near copies | a variant is a truncated LinkedIn post |
| 3 | First line | the idea reads before the fold on every network | one network opens on context | the point appears after the fold |
| 4 | Link | the pointer names the right place and the comment or reply says what the link is | the reply holds a bare URL with no line before it | the reader cannot tell where the link is |
| 5 | Media | each network gets the right file (PDF on LinkedIn, slides in order on Instagram, the cover on X), every image has a useful alt text | an alt text is vague | a network gets a file it cannot show, or the wrong image |
| 6 | Voice | the founder's voice and the project's decisions (memory) hold in every variant | one variant drifts in tone | a variant breaks a recorded decision |

Return per post: the scores, the located problems (`<id>`, the sentence), one proposed fix each. No rewriting: the producer revises once through `posts.json`.
