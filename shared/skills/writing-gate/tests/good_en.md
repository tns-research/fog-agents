# Invoices that get paid on time

Freelancers on our pilot send an invoice from the project page in two clicks. The client gets a payment link, and you see when they open it.

Late payment usually starts before the invoice: the quote never said when payment is due. So the quote template now asks for a due date and copies it onto the invoice.

We tested the reminder schedule with twelve pilot users in September. Reminders at day 3 and day 10 got answers; a reminder at day 1 annoyed clients, so we removed it.
